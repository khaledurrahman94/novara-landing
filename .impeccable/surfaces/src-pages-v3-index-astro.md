# /v3 surface brief

**Scope:** the V3 landing page at `/v3`. **Mode:** Persuade.

This is a bolder, hands-on extension of the established V2 world (DESIGN.md), pinned by the user. It has more parallax, cursor-following, interactivity, and scroll-scrubbed reversible animation, and it goes deeper on the AI interview's integrity checks.

**Audience and action:** as for V2. Request a demo from the nav and the close, with no hero form. **Proof:** the real round, plus hands-on product demos with illustrative data.

**Memorable moment:** the visitor tries to cheat the live interview demo and gets their own timestamped integrity report.

**Unresolved:**
- the demo-form endpoint;
- the AI interviewer avatar media.

## Direction contract

THESIS: V2's page made hands-on. Every feature can be touched, dragged, toggled or tested, and the interview integrity checks run live on the visitor. It refuses motion that only decorates, and it refuses narrative.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited from DESIGN.md: white and #F9FAFB bands, a hairline-framed 1200px column, Geist, violet only on actions and data, the gradient once behind the hero, product UI in Inter. Added: a contextual cursor pill and spring-physical, interruptible interactions.

STORY: The visitor:
- pulls the review screen apart and reassembles it;
- watches 1,200 dots become 4;
- re-ranks resumes by toggling requirements;
- tries to cheat the AI interview and reads their own integrity report;
- makes the shortlist call by dragging;
- requests a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: In the upper 40%, a left-aligned H1 "Hire the person, not the pile.", the sub-line, and a "Try the AI interview" link. Below, the candidate-review screen in exploded depth on the gradient horizon: its layers and evidence chips float at different depths, follow the cursor, and reassemble as you scroll.

FORM: a user-pinned extension of the established V2 world. No concept roll was run, because the user pinned the direction ("I love what you have done with the design language and consistency… slightly more wilder").

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
