# /v2 surface brief

**Scope:** the V2 landing page at `/v2`. **Mode:** Persuade.

**Audience:** hiring teams at high-volume employers. **Action:** request a demo, using the working email form in the hero and again at the close. **Proof:** one real hiring round for engineering roles (1,200+ applied, 600 AI interviews, 12 shortlisted, 4 hired) and the rebuilt product UI. **Constraints:** PRODUCT.md claims only, the binding palette, a standard page with no metaphor or narrative.

**Memorable moment:** the hero's candidate-review screen flattens from its tilt as you scroll, and its panels come alive: the score counts up, the criteria tick, and the integrity event appears.

**Unresolved:**
- the demo-form endpoint;
- real footage of the AI interviewer avatar;
- the launch domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A conventional B2B landing page finished to Linear and Stripe level, with Attio's lightness. The product proves itself section by section. It refuses metaphor and narrative, and it refuses the agency's floating-card collage and repeated feature grids.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with bg (#F9FAFB) plates. A full-height pair of 1px border-secondary hairlines frames a centred column, and hairlines divide the sections. Ink is text-primary. Violet primary appears only on actions and data. The brand gradient appears once, as the horizon behind the hero product shot, never behind text. Type is Geist. The imagery is rebuilt Novara product UI in Inter.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor knows that Novara screens resumes, runs AI first rounds and shows the evidence. They see the real round 1,200+ → 4, see each step as product UI, have their objections answered in the FAQ, and request a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Upper 45%: a left-aligned eyebrow, the H1 "Hire the person, not the pile.", a sub-line, and an inline email field with a "Request a demo" button. Below it, a full-column candidate-review screen tilted about 10° in perspective, its top 40% above the fold on the gradient horizon.

FORM: the category standard (canon), chosen by the user as the standing exit; seed:283621d478fc3176

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
