# Home page surface brief

**Scope:** the landing page at `/`. **Mode:** Persuade.

It is a hands-on page in the world DESIGN.md describes. It has parallax, cursor-following, interactivity, and scroll-scrubbed reversible animation, and it goes deeper on the AI interview's integrity checks.

**Audience and action:** hiring teams evaluating the platform. Request a demo from the nav and the close, with no hero form. **Proof:** the real round, plus hands-on product demos with illustrative data.

**Memorable moment:** the visitor tries to cheat the live interview demo and gets their own timestamped integrity report.

**Unresolved:**
- the demo-form endpoint;
- the AI interviewer avatar media.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a standard landing page made hands-on. Every feature can be touched, dragged, toggled or tested, and the interview integrity checks run live on the visitor. It refuses motion that only decorates, and it refuses narrative.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited from DESIGN.md: white and #F9FAFB bands, a hairline-framed 1200px column, Geist, violet only on actions and data, the gradient once behind the hero, product UI in Inter. Added: a contextual cursor pill and spring-physical, interruptible interactions.

STORY: The visitor:
- pulls the review screen apart and reassembles it;
- watches 1,200 dots become 4;
- re-ranks resumes by toggling requirements;
- tries to cheat the AI interview and reads their own integrity report;
- makes the shortlist call by dragging;
- requests a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: In the upper 40%, a left-aligned H1 "Hire the person, not the pile.", the sub-line, and a "Try the AI interview" link. Below, the candidate-review screen in exploded depth on the gradient horizon: its layers and evidence chips float at different depths, follow the cursor, and reassemble as you scroll.

FORM: a standard landing page with a hands-on interaction layer. No concept roll was run.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
